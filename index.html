06 // THE STRUCTURA PROTOCOL

ZERO-DEFECT EXECUTION

PHASE 01

Geotechnical & 5D BIM Coordination

Borehole soil sonic testing, structural loading clash detection in 5D BIM, and procurement of long-lead substation transformers and high-tensile rebar before initial groundbreaking.

PHASE 02

Substructure & Deep Foundations

Secant piling, continuous flight auger (CFA) caissons anchored into bedrock, slurry wall retention, and mass concrete base slab pours monitored via wireless thermal sensors.

PHASE 03

Superstructure & Envelope Enclosure

Self-performed slipform cores, structural steel erection utilizing twin high-capacity luffing jib tower cranes, and robotic curtain-wall facade bracket installation at 3-day cycle rates.

PHASE 04

Commissioning & Handover Guarantee

Full MEP integrated systems pressure testing, acoustic certification, air leakage thermal imaging, digital BIM as-built twin delivery, and our standard 24-month structural warranty.
